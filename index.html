I'm a creative technologist and image maker, working at the intersection of design, emergent technology and cultural zeitgeist.

I am particularly interested in what all our new tools mean for a creative practise, for culture and people, where they fit into our world meaningfully, and where they don't. I investigate this curiosity through my studio practise, using the very mediums of our current digital age to reveal their inner workings including code, LLM models, 3D, interactive and experience design.

My curiosities have instigated opportunities within Tech and Fashion, collaborating with the likes of Nick Knight, Converse, Google's Creative Lab, SXSW festival and London Tech Week as well as being published by Dazed, The Cold Magazine and ZHUANGSHI Journal.
